About / Philosophy

“Knowledge doesn’t save lives. Behavior does.”

I am a Learning & Development Manager, Learning Experience Designer, and Serious Game Developer based in İzmir, Türkiye. Over the past decade, my work has focused on transforming mission-critical training from passive compliance checklists into living, consequential simulations.

The Journey

My pedagogical foundation began with an academic background in English Language Teaching & Applied Linguistics from Uludağ University. Teaching complex communication and cognitive patterns to adult learners taught me an indelible lesson: true competence is not measured by multiple-choice recall, but by situational judgment under pressure.

Encountering the global Articulate community was a defining pivot. Recognizing that traditional e-learning authoring tools frequently limited authentic exploration, I began bridging instructional system design with custom frontend engines—integrating JavaScript, 2D Canvas physics solvers, WebGL shaders, and game design state machines directly into browser environments.

At Maritime Trainer (MTR), I lead learning and development initiatives for global maritime operations. In high-consequence industries where improper watchkeeping or ballast miscalculations risk human lives and millions of dollars, static training fails. My team designs zero-latency kinetic labs (ship stability, hydrodynamics, radar collision avoidance, enclosed-space gas hazards) and motion-comic scenario architectures that enable seafarers to test physical boundaries safely.

In parallel, as an active design volunteer and mentor with GamFed Türkiye (International Gamification Federation), I advocate for purposeful game systems, visceral combat/game feel, and experiential learning models.
